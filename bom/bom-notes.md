# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Item numbers match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`) and the components table in WWK-PRC-001.

- Without assist (items 1 to 8 and 12), parts total about $300, within the $450 concept budget in `project.yaml`, leaving about $150 for tools, spares and rework.
- With the optional assist (items 9 to 11), parts total about $600, which exceeds the budget. The recommendation is to leave assist out of the first prototype. Raising the budget is not proposed here; any change is for Amish to decide.
- The SwapCell pack (item 10) may be shared with the SwapCell project, which would remove about $150 from this repo's cost.
- The jerrycans (item 6) are the users' own containers and are not purchased.
- All wear parts are standard 26 in bicycle parts so they can be bought and repaired in rural markets (R12).
